# My Favorite Movie: The Lorax

## About the Movie

**The Lorax** is one of my favorite animated movies. It tells the story of a boy named Ted who wants to find a real tree. Along the way, he learns about the Lorax, a creature who speaks for the trees and protects nature.

The movie teaches us about the *importance of trees, nature, and taking care of our environment.*

## Main Characters

Here are some of the main characters:

- **The Lorax** - The guardian of the trees who speaks for nature.
- **Ted Wiggins** - The boy who wants to find a real tree.
- **The Once-ler** - The man who cuts down the trees to make a business.
- **Audrey** - The girl who loves trees and nature.
- **Mrs. Wiggins** - Ted's mother.

## My Favorite Parts

1. When Ted discovers the story of the Lorax.
2. When the Lorax tries to protect the trees.
3. When Ted works hard to bring trees back.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like The Lorax because it has a meaningful story and colorful characters. It reminds us that we should protect nature and not destroy the environment just for money. It also teaches us that even one person can make a difference.

## My Favorite Quote

> "Unless someone like you cares a whole awful lot, nothing is going to get better. It's not."
